PROJECT 01 REX HÖREN INDUSTRIES ARIFA HACKATHON 3RD PRIZE

Vibrawave – The Hearing Aid

Tactile acoustic conversion via cranial trigeminal nerve stimulation.

Vibrawave is an assistive technology concept designed to convert sound information into tactile vibration signals through trigeminal nerve stimulation rather than conventional hearing amplification or bone conduction.

Legal & Clinical Note: Engineering proof-of-concept prototype. Does not claim clinical validation or medical device certification.
